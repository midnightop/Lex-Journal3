CLAT 2027 Overview

The Consortium of National Law Universities (NLUs) releases the CLAT 2027 syllabus PDF on its official website. The syllabus is highly essential for students preparing for the undergraduate and postgraduate law entrance examinations.

The exam will primarily focus on evaluating the comprehension and reasoning skills and abilities of candidates. It is designed to be a test of aptitude and skills that are necessary for a legal education rather than prior knowledge.

Subjects-wise Syllabus 2027

The CLAT UG 2027 syllabus comprises five major sections. Candidates must prepare thoroughly for each to secure a rank in the top NLUs.

  • English Language: Passages derived from contemporary or historically significant fiction and non-fiction writing.
  • Current Affairs & General Knowledge: News, journalistic sources, and other non-fiction writing.
  • Legal Reasoning: Fact situations or scenarios involving legal matters, public policy questions, or moral philosophical enquiries.
  • Logical Reasoning: Short passages followed by questions assessing your ability to analyze patterns.
  • Quantitative Techniques: Short sets of facts or propositions, graphs, or other textual, pictorial, or diagrammatic representations.

Eligibility Criteria

There is no upper age limit for candidates appearing for the UG Programme through the CLAT 2027 examination.

As regards minimum percentage of marks in the qualifying examination (i.e., 10+2 or an equivalent examination), the candidates must have secured:

• Forty five percent (45%) marks or its equivalent grade in case of candidates belonging to General / OBC / PWD / NRI / PIO / OCI categories.
• Forty Percent (40%) marks or equivalent in case of candidates belonging to SC/ST categories.

Admit Card Details

The Admit Card/Hall Ticket for CLAT 2027 will be available for download on the official portal a few weeks before the exam date. It is a mandatory document. No candidate will be allowed to enter the examination hall without a valid admit card and a government-issued photo ID.
