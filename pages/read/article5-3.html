Salary DNA: The 50% Rule Explained

The transition to India's **4 New Labour Codes** marks a pivotal shift. The "Code on Wages" fundamentally changes how your monthly salary is calculated. This is no longer just a corporate decision; it is a legal mandate.

Basic Pay Cap

Your Basic Salary must now be at least 50% of your total CTC. This eliminates "ghost allowances" used to lower PF contributions.

The PF (Provident Fund) Surge

Since PF is a percentage of Basic Pay, a higher Basic Pay means significantly higher savings. You are essentially forced to build a larger retirement fund.

Reduced In-Hand Salary

As PF contributions increase, your monthly net pay (take-home) might see a slight dip. It is a shift from present liquidity to future security.

Hours & Overtime in 2026

The 2026 framework introduces flexibility that could lead to longer weekends or longer workdays. The weekly limit remains 48 hours, but distribution can change.

The 4-Day Work Week Option

  • 12-Hour Shifts: Companies can implement 12-hour shifts for 4 days, followed by 3 days off.
  • Overtime Pay: Any hour worked beyond 48 hours weekly must be paid at 2x the normal wage.
  • Consent: These changes generally require mutual consent and cannot be forced overnight.

Employee FAQs

Is the 4-day work week mandatory in India?

No. It is an option. If adopted, the working day will extend to 12 hours to maintain the 48-hour weekly legal limit.

How will this affect my PF (Provident Fund)?

Because Basic Pay must be 50% of your CTC, the base for PF increases. Both employee and employer contributions will rise, resulting in a larger retirement corpus.

Can women work night shifts under new codes?

Yes. The OSH Code allows women to work in all establishments, including night shifts, provided the employer ensures safety, transport, and consent.
