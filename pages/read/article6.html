Justice at Your Fingertips

In 2026, the distance between a victim and justice is no longer measured in kilometers to the nearest station, but in centimeters on your screen. With the implementation of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), digital FIRs are now a fundamental right.

The 12-Minute Workflow

Phase 1: Digital KYC

Log in via OTP verified against the National Citizen Database. This creates a permanent, non-repudiable link between the complainant and the report.

Phase 2: Narrative & Geo-Tagging

Drop a pin on the digital map. Precise coordinates prevent jurisdictional disputes and force the system to assign an Investigating Officer (IO) immediately.

Phase 3: The Digital Hash Vault

Upload screenshots, photos, or transaction IDs. The portal generates a Digital Hash Value for each file, ensuring evidence cannot be tampered with later.

Zero FIR & Section 173 BNSS

Under the 2026 protocol, the Zero FIR is fully mandatory. If you file a report on the Kolkata portal for an incident in Mumbai, the system must accept it. Refusal by an officer to register a Zero FIR is now a punishable offense.

The Power of Section 173 (BNSS):

This section explicitly recognizes 'Information in electronic form.' It mandates that a signed copy of the FIR must be provided to the informant immediately—which in the digital era means an instant, e-signed PDF download.

Why Digital beats Paper?

  • Cloud Residency: Once a number is generated on the State Cloud, it cannot be deleted without a High Court order.
  • Audit Trail: Every second of delay in assigning an officer is tracked by senior IPS officials in real-time.

Expert FAQs

Can I file for a lost Aadhaar or PAN Card?

Yes. These are 'Non-Cognizable' reports. The instant online receipt is legally valid for applying for duplicates.

Is a Digital FIR valid for court evidence?

Absolutely. Under the 2026 evidence rules, an e-signed FIR with a digital hash certificate carries the same weight as a physical one.

What happens if I give false info?

Under Section 217 of the BNS, framing someone via false FIR is a serious crime. The digital footprint makes it 100% traceable to you.
